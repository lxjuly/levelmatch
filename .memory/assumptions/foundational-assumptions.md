# Foundational Assumptions

## Portfolio Projects Convert Better Than Tutorials

Hiring managers for AI roles evaluate shipped work over certifications or coursework. Three distinct shipped projects (aifund, chronelle, LevelMatch) is enough to start conversations.

## Self-Referential Tools Signal Product Instinct

Building a job analyzer to help land an AI job demonstrates both domain expertise and product thinking — a more legible signal than a generic AI demo.

## LLM Extraction Is Sufficient For Gap Analysis

Extracting structured signals (required skills, level, tech stack, compensation range) from raw job posting text via LLM is accurate enough for gap analysis without needing purpose-built NLP or fine-tuning at MVP.

## Production AI Experience Outweighs Portfolio Depth

The project steward's current work building a self-service analytics agent provides production credibility (RAG, evals, real users) that is more valuable to interviewers than additional portfolio surface area. LevelMatch complements rather than substitutes for that experience.

## The Job Market Is Analyzable From Public Postings

Enough signal about skill demand, seniority expectations, and compensation is derivable from publicly available job postings without needing proprietary recruiter data.

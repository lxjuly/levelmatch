# LevelMatch Is The AI Job Market Analyzer

LevelMatch is the project steward's AI Job Market Analyzer portfolio piece.

The core product: ingest job postings, extract structured signals via LLM (required skills, experience level, tech stack, compensation), and surface a gap analysis against the user's background.

The name "LevelMatch" reflects the core value proposition — matching a candidate's level to the market's expectations, not just a keyword matcher.

This was chosen over alternatives (tutorial projects, isolated RAG demos) because it is self-referential (building an AI tool to find an AI job), ships as a real product with a clear user, and generates content for September write-ups.

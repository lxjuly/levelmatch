# Prep Plan Follow-Through

The platform helps the candidate follow through the prep plan.

**Why:** Plans fail without follow-through; this is where outcomes and retention are
won. Fourth step of [[two-sided-matching-loop]].

**Depends on:** [[prep-plan-generation]].

**Claims to validate:**
- Lightweight follow-through (reminders, check-ins, progress tracking) materially
  improves plan completion.
- Candidates will engage with an ongoing tool, not just a one-time report.

**Main fork:** passive tracking (the candidate self-reports) vs. active agentic
coaching (the platform nudges and adapts the plan). This is where LevelMatch becomes
agentic.

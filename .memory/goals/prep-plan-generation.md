# Prep Plan Generation

From the revised resume and a matched job, the platform proposes a prep plan for the
candidate.

**Why:** Bridges the gap from "matched" to "ready" — the candidate's concrete path to
the role. Third step of [[two-sided-matching-loop]].

**Depends on:** [[resume-upgrade-suggestions]].

**Claims to validate:**
- Identified gaps map to a credible, time-bound prep plan.
- Candidates want a plan, not just a gap list.

**Main fork:** skill-acquisition plan vs. interview-prep plan vs. both. (Likely both,
weighted by the size of the gap.)

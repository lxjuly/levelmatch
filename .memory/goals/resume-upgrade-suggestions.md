# Resume Upgrade Suggestions

The platform proposes concrete resume upgrades using the job data it has gathered.

**Why:** Turns aggregated market demand into actionable candidate improvement, tying
the gap analysis directly back to the resume. Second step of
[[two-sided-matching-loop]].

**Depends on:** [[resume-upload-matching]] + gap analyzer + market aggregates.

**Claims to validate:**
- Aggregated market demand is specific enough to drive concrete, credible resume edits.
- Candidates trust and act on machine-proposed resume changes.

**Main fork:** per-target-job tailoring vs. market-wide upgrade suggestions. (Likely
both — a general pass plus per-job tailoring.)

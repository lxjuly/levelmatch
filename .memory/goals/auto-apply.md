# Automated Application

Once the candidate is ready, the platform applies to the matched job automatically.

**Why:** Closes the loop and removes the last friction between a prepared candidate
and the role. Fifth and final step of [[two-sided-matching-loop]].

**Depends on:** [[prep-plan-followthrough]].

**Claims to validate:**
- Auto-apply is acceptable to candidates (consent) and effective with employers.
- It improves outcomes rather than adding noise to employers' pipelines.

**Constraints (this one has real bounds):**
- Job-board / ATS terms of service often restrict automated submission.
- Explicit candidate consent per application is required.
- Anti-spam / two-sided-benefit: mass auto-applying undermines the hiring-manager
  side of [[two-sided-matching-loop]]. The loop only works if applications stay
  high-quality and well-matched.

**Main fork:** assisted-apply (platform pre-fills, candidate reviews and submits) vs.
fully automatic. The constraints push toward assisted-apply first.
